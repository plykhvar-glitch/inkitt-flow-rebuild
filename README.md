# Growth Audit — Inkitt Galatea

**Live page: https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/inkitt-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the readers Galatea already has, plus a clickable prototype
of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Galatea's own design: the welcome, the trope quiz, the
loader, a first shelf, the Unlimited paywall, an offer on close and the notification ask. Pick
different tropes and every screen after the quiz changes. English, German and Portuguese, light
and dark, a 30-second walkthrough, and a QR code to try it on a phone.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Today | In the prototype | Expected impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Catch the close with the 110-point pack | The X on Unlimited goes back to the book | A sheet offers the Recommended 110-point pack at $7.99 | ARPU +10–15% |
| 2 | Show Unlimited after the quiz, named after her tropes | First seen at a locked chapter, after sign-up: "Unlock unlimited access to all stories" | Right after the first shelf: "Unlock unlimited Enemies to Lovers & Bad Boys" | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | Let the loader and a first shelf use the quiz | "Evaluating all fictional crushes" for everyone | The loader names her tropes; a shelf of Galatea's own titles from those sections | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | The prompt fires on Continue from the quiz | After the paywall, behind "Never Miss New Releases and Updates" | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | Put the 299K ratings next to the price | One quote and a small "5 million readers" line | 4.6 from 299K App Store ratings and 15 million women, above the plans | CR +10–15% |

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps —
not measured lift for Inkitt.*

Prices, point packs, the rating, the reader counts, the quote, the tropes, the book titles and
covers come from Galatea's own in-app screens, galatea.com and its App Store listing.
